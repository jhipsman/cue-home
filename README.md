# Cue Home

A home screen for Google TV and Android TV, built on Cue: the remote's Home
button opens it, and from it you browse everything on your streaming
services, Cue's own films and shows, and Live TV in one place, then open
the right app at the title (or play it in Cue).

Early days: this starts as a copy of Cue's Fire TV / Google TV app (from
jhipsman/cueapp, android/), which it grows from. TV-APP-NOTES.md is that
app's README, kept for its build and test notes.

Planned for the first version:

- Opens on Home when the TV starts and when Home is pressed (a one-time,
  undoable setup turns Google's home screen off).
- Continue watching, from Cue and, where the device lets a home screen read
  Android's Watch Next list, from Netflix and the other apps too.
- Rows across your services, Top 10s, Live TV on now, an Apps row and All
  apps; a clock (and weather) along the top.
- Picking a title opens the service's app at it, or plays it in Cue.
